Iron Tundra

New in 4.7: Iron Tundra runs and its launcher (an invented, mouse-only window) is on screen. Home shows the game's card: Resume with the pointer (the first time, its buttons are explained once). In the launcher: left stick moves the pointer, D-pad small steps, A click, X right click, right stick scrolls, Y types (the console's keyboard, then the text goes to the window). Play starts the game: the pointer turns off by itself. B on Home opens the quick menu: Controls has the pointer too.
Keyboard: W A S D = left stick, arrows = D-pad, Enter = A, Esc = B, X, Y, M = Menu, Page Up / Down = right stick, H = Guide (Home).
Demo only: R forget "Don't show again" and start again, P palette, T text size, G button prompts, L language, N sounds (on). Address: #lang=it&open=home|home-on|explain|app|pointer|typing|controls|game
